Read what's on the P-6

The P-6 only shows its sounds when it exports them, one bank at a time. Nothing is loaded into your kit: you'll see what's there and how it compares.

  1. Switch the P-6 off and connect it by USB.
  2. Hold the bank's button, [A/E] to [D/H], and switch it on. For banks E–H, hold [SAMPLING] as well.
  3. Wait about a minute while the step buttons light up, until the P-6 drive appears.
  4. Pick the drive (or its EXPORT folder).

Edits made on the P-6 only show up if they were saved there first ([SHIFT] + [TIE] (WRITE)).

Write to the P-6

  1. Connect the P-6 by USB, hold [SAMPLING] and switch it on.
  2. Choose what to write, then pick the P-6 drive (or its IMPORT folder) when asked.
  3. Eject the drive, then press [KYBD] on the P-6 to import. Wait for donE.
